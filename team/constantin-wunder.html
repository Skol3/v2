Biography

International Football Agent & Strategic Partner

With more than a decade of experience in the international football industry, Constantin Wunder has built a strong professional network across Europe and South America, developing relationships with clubs, players, agents and key decision-makers within the global football ecosystem.

As a FIFA-licensed football agent based in Germany, Constantin specializes in identifying opportunities, building international bridges and creating strategic pathways for players and clubs.

His work combines a deep understanding of the European football market with a long-term vision focused on sustainable sporting and professional development.

Together with Argentina Sport Stars, Constantin works to connect South American talent with international opportunities, creating a structured bridge between Argentina and the European football market.

Biography

Co-Founder& Director of International Relations

Emanuel Cedrón is the co-founder of Argentina Sport Stars and a key figure in the development of the agency’s international network and strategic partnerships.

With a strong background in scouting, international relations and business development, Emanuel specializes in identifying opportunities and creating meaningful connections between people, clubs and organizations within the football industry.

His greatest strength lies in his ability to build relationships. Through a natural understanding of people, direct communication and a strong entrepreneurial mindset, Emanuel develops bridges between the Argentine football ecosystem and international markets.

Within Argentina Sport Stars, he is responsible for identifying promising talent, establishing relationships with clubs and decision-makers, developing strategic partnerships and opening new international opportunities for players and organizations.

His role goes beyond traditional scouting. Emanuel combines a deep understanding of the football environment with a strong ability to connect people, understand different perspectives and transform relationships into concrete opportunities.

Together with Constantin Wunder, he forms the foundation of Argentina Sport Stars: combining European experience and international football expertise with direct access to Argentina’s rich football ecosystem.

His vision is clear: to create sustainable international connections, identify the right opportunities and help build long-term value for players, clubs and partners.

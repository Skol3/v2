Футбол • Спортивный бизнес • Нетворкинг • Международные связи

Мероприятие FUSSBALL KONGRESS 2026
Дата 7 октября 2026
Место SpOrt Stuttgart, Stuttgart
Представитель ASS Constantin Wunder
ARGENTINA SPORT STARS

Константин Вундер на FUSSBALL KONGRESS в Штутгарте — 2026

Constantin Wunder, international football agent and Co-Founder of Argentina Sport Stars, attended the FUSSBALL KONGRESS 2026 in Штутгарт, Германия, bringing together professionals, decision-makers and leading figures from football, sport and business.

The 42nd edition of the FUSSBALL KONGRESS took place on 7 октября 2026 at SpOrt Stuttgart, located in the city's NeckarPark. The congress focused on important topics shaping the future of football and the wider sports business, including leadership, team culture, artificial intelligence, social responsibility, resilience and personal branding.

Для Argentina Sport Stars участие в профессиональных мероприятиях, подобных FUSSBALL KONGRESS, — важная часть поддержания международных связей, обмена идеями и общения с людьми и организациями, которые формируют современную футбольную индустрию.

Хайденхайм — Хольштайн Киль

Матч на «Фойт-Арене» • Немецкий футбол • Международные связи

МАТЧ 1. FC Heidenheim 1 – 0 Holstein Kiel
ДАТА 13 сентября 2026
ТУРНИР Немецкий футбол
СТАДИОН Voith-Arena, Heidenheim, Germany
ARGENTINA SPORT STARS

Константин Вундер на матче Хайденхайм — Хольштайн Киль

Constantin Wunder, international football agent and Co-Founder of Argentina Sport Stars, attended the Bundesliga 2 fixture between 1. FC Heidenheim and Holstein Kiel at the Voith-Arena in Heidenheim, Germany.

The match offered another opportunity to experience German professional football from inside the stadium, follow the atmosphere around the fixture and stay connected with the football environment in Germany.

Heidenheim secured a 1–0 home victory after a competitive encounter. Following a goalless first half, the decisive moment came in the 84th minute when Jonas Föhrenbach scored following a corner to give the home side all three points.

For Argentina Sport Stars, attending professional football matches is an important part of maintaining international football connections, following the development of the game and building relationships across the European football market.

ДЕНЬ МАТЧА

Футбольный день в Хайденхайме

01

На «Фойт-Арене»

The match took place at a sold-out Voith-Arena, providing a strong atmosphere for an important German football fixture. Being present at the stadium offered a direct view of the environment surrounding professional football in Germany.

02

Напряжённый первый тайм

The first half ended without a goal, with both teams competing in a closely contested match. The score remained 0–0 going into the interval.

03

Решающий момент

The breakthrough came late in the game. In the 84th minute, Jonas Föhrenbach converted from a corner to put Heidenheim ahead and ultimately secure a 1–0 home victory.

04

Футбол за пределами матча

For Argentina Sport Stars, matchday visits are about more than watching the final score. They are opportunities to remain connected with clubs, players and football professionals while continuing to develop an international football network.

МАТЧ VfR Aalen 2 – 0 SSV Ulm 1846
ДАТА 8 сентября 2026
ТУРНИР Региональная лига «Юго-Запад»
СТАДИОН «Бедиани-Арена», Аален, Германия
ARGENTINA SPORT STARS

Константин Вундер на матче Аален — Ульм

Constantin Wunder, international football agent and co-founder of Argentina Sport Stars, attended the Региональная лига «Юго-Запад» clash between VfR Aalen and SSV Ulm 1846 in Aalen, Germany.

The evening brought together two traditional clubs in an intense regional derby, with the atmosphere around the Bediani-Arena reflecting the passion and character of German football. With thousands of supporters in attendance, the match offered a strong example of the energy, commitment and competitive spirit that define football at this level.

For Argentina Sport Stars, attending live matches is an important part of maintaining strong international football connections. Being present at stadiums allows our team to experience the football environment first-hand, follow players and clubs closely, and continue developing relationships within the international football network.

ДЕНЬ МАТЧА

Футбольный вечер в Аалене

01

Атмосфера дерби

From the build-up to kick-off, the Bediani-Arena was filled with the atmosphere of a genuine regional derby. A total of 4,821 spectators attended the match, creating an impressive setting for the evening.

02

Аален выходит вперёд

VfR Aalen opened the scoring in the 37th minute. Dean Melo found the net to give the home side a 1–0 advantage before the break.

03

Решающий момент

Ulm continued to look for a way back into the match after the interval, but Aalen remained dangerous on the counter. In the 84th minute, Dean Melo scored his second goal of the evening to make it 2–0 and secure the home victory.

04

Футбол не ограничивается 90 минутами

For Argentina Sport Stars, matchdays are about more than the final score. They are opportunities to stay connected with clubs, players, coaches and football professionals, while continuing to observe the game from inside the football environment.

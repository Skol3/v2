ABOUT SANTIAGO CESANELLI

Santiago Cesanelli is an experienced Argentine centre forward recognized for his intelligent movement, composure inside the penalty area and consistent goal-scoring ability. Throughout his professional career, he has demonstrated strong attacking instincts and a natural ability to create dangerous situations in front of goal.

His style of play combines mobility, technical quality and determination. Santiago is capable of playing both as a traditional striker and within a dynamic attacking system, contributing with finishing, link-up play and relentless work ethic.

Argentina Sport Stars proudly represents Santiago Cesanelli, supporting his professional career while creating new international opportunities and helping him reach the next stage of his football journey.
